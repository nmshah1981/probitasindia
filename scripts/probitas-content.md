Design Projects by Leadership

List of few Commercial & Infra projects executed during professional Journey
LTI Mahape:  (13.72 lakh sq.ft. Building to be handed over in 1 year) Design and Build Project by L&T. This was a commercial tower with 2B+3S+12 floors. It was a completely Structural steel beam column system with structural steel cores.

Air India Engine Test Cell Facility, at Nagpur for CENCO- Safran Group: The facility is capable of testing: GE90-115B engines initially with GEnx capability in the future. Project included an extremely complex Concrete Test cell structure where Aircraft Engine Blast loads are anticipated and related Ancillary Buildings.

Air India MRO Hangar at Nagpur, for client Boeing: This was the largest MRO Facility in India consisted of 200 m wide Structural steel aircraft Hangar and related Ancillary buildings capable of servicing largest aircrafts like Airbus A380 and Boeing 777 & 747

Seawoods Grand central, Commercial & Railway Station: This is India’s largest Transit oriented Development (TOD)  that included development of Railways station with Commercial building and Retail Facility. In addition to other Utility Building & ancillary structures.

Mumbai International Airport. This was a complete design and build project for reconstruction of existing Mumbai Airport. The scope included Terminal building, related ancillary buildings and Air side works like roads, Runways, taxiways, Aprons and other related works for Mumbai Airport

Design Projects by Leadership

List of few of Residential projects executed during professional Journey
Tata Housing (Aveza/Gateway Towers) at Mulund, Mumbai: 160 m Tall, 6 towers : G + 4 P + Eco Deck + 32 to 42 Floors.
The towers use “cut-outs” and “sky-bridges” to create visual relief and framed vista; thus, it is not a monolithic block and makes it a complex structure to design.

Sahana-Sheth Creators (Beau Monte) at Sion, Mumbai: 2 towers, each with 1B + G+ 5 P + Eco Deck + 40 Floors.
with 187 mts. Height, it is Tallest ultra-luxury tower in Sion with a robust central core; and Wings are inclined to mitigate wind forces.

DLF (One Midtown) at Moti Nagar, New Delhi: 4 towers, each with 3B + G + 2 P + 39 Floors.
Designed for Seismic Zone V as per client requirements for enhanced safety.

Kalpataru (Vista) at Noida: 2 towers, each with 2B + 2 P + E-deck + 33 Floors. Friction piles were adopted as the subsoil predominantly consists of sandy soil.

Dheeraj Realty (LivSmart at Kurla, Mumbai): 28 towers each with 3B + G + 14Floors. Originally designed as commercial towers, later converted to residential, while 2 basements were already constructed.

Mahindra Lifespace Developers Ltd. (Happinest) Tathawade, Pune: 3 towers with 1B + G + 32 floors and 3 towers with 1B + G + 22 floors.

Rustomjee (Prive) at Bandra, Mumbai: 2 towers, each with 2B + G + 4 P +16 floors. Extensive floating system adopted due to space limitations.

Sardar Height, Gujarat Housing Board, Valsad: 28 Blocks, each with G +14 floors.
